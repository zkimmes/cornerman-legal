Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?
Open the Shop (or Settings) in the game and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel a membership?
On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Cage Legend.

How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my progress saved?
On your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your career.

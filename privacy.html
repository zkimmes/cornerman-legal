Privacy Policy

Cage Legend: MMA Manager · Last updated 16 November 2026

Cage Legend is a single-player game. We built it to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what the app does with information.

What stays on your device

Your fighter, career, settings and game progress are saved only on your iPhone. We do not run game servers and we cannot see your save. If you delete the app, this data is deleted.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To check what you have bought and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created by the app (not your name, email or Apple ID) and the record of your purchases. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in, name or email is required.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Children

Cage Legend is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can delete all game data by deleting the app. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions. To ask about the purchase identifier RevenueCat holds, or to ask for it to be deleted, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
